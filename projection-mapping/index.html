Projection Mapping for Architecture, Brands and Culture

ANDATA LAB designs and produces projection mapping experiences from concept and facade study to 3D content, real-time systems and on-site calibration.

Immersive experiences, engineered.

What we produce

A projection mapping studio for built space.

We treat architecture as the canvas: heritage facades, museums, brands and cultural events. The work is site-specific — measured, modelled and calibrated to the real surface, then operated as a show.

When the brief calls for it, mapping can sit inside a wider experiential system: sensor-driven interactive installations, museum exhibits, fulldome and 360° content, or permanent hospitality environments.

Architectural projection mapping

Facade study, 3D content and on-site warping for buildings and heritage architecture.

Interactive projection mapping

Real-time playback that can respond to music, sensors or audience movement when the project requires it.

Cultural and heritage storytelling

Narratives written for a place — local myth, ceremony and architecture held together in one mapped sequence.

Brand and product experiences

Mapped environments for launches, stages and spatial brand moments, produced as installed shows.

3D and motion content

Modelled and animated content matched to the geometry of the surface, from still studies to full motion sequences.

TouchDesigner and real-time playback

Show control and playback in TouchDesigner — cues, warping and live operation, not only pre-rendered files.

Spatial audio and show control

Spatial audio and interactive sound designed with the picture, plus the control layer that runs the night.

Projector alignment, warping and calibration

On-site alignment to irregular architecture so the image sits on cornices, windows and brick — not beside them.

Case study · Immersive wellness

A theater that breathes with the class.

For Asana Yoga at Conjunto Santander, ANDATA LAB built a generative light column and a projected canopy that moved with the music, the instructor and hundreds of people on their mats.

Overview

The practice, extended into the room.

The brief was to turn a performing-arts theater into an immersive practice space — not a screen behind the class, but a room the class could inhabit.

ANDATA LAB built a fabric column projected on four sides at the center of the mats, plus a canopy of cloth over the hall as a second surface. Generative visuals shifted color and tempo with each block of the session.

The space

One column, one sky.

Two projection surfaces: a four-faced fabric column in the center of the room, and a canopy overhead that turned the ceiling into sky.

Creative approach

Not a backdrop. Part of the practice.

01

Visual direction

Palettes by class block — calm, energy, close. Organic language: water, fluids, particles, flowers. The image follows the instructor, not a loop behind her.

02

Generative engine

TouchDesigner in real time, audio-reactive, never the same twice. The system listens to the music and the room rather than playing a fixed timeline.

03

Multi-surface mapping

Four faces of the column plus the canopy, mapped and mixed from Resolume Arena so both surfaces stay one picture.

04

Live operation

Show control through the full session — cues tracking the instructor and the live music, from first breath to close.

What if the resort could sense, respond and remember?

A resort is not only architecture. It can become a living interface between guests, the destination, environmental data and cultural memory.

The guest does not observe the artwork. The guest completes it.

Data art

A named condition becomes a behavior in the room.

Data art is a translation. Wind has a direction. The moon has a phase. The sea has a temperature. The guest has a body. Each one can move light — if we name it, and wire it honestly.

Not a decorative loop “inspired by nature.” Proposed, viable inputs from the destination — confirmed per property before anything is connected.

  1. PlaceThis coast, this hour, this architecture.
  2. DataTide, wind, moon, sea temperature, species, presence.
  3. BehaviorDrift, breathe, densify, attract, appear.
  4. RoomWalls, floor, water and façade become the canvas.
  5. GuestCompletes it. Does not walk past a screen.
Place→Data→Behavior→Visual system→Guest memory

Permanent amenity

Portrait of Place

A responsive social-memory installation. Guest presence becomes a living portrait of the destination — meaningful in the room, then optionally carried beyond it.

  1. A guest approaches the display.
  2. A discreet depth sensor reads silhouette, position and movement — not identity.
  3. Live particles, flora and local species gather around the body.
  4. The portrait evolves with time of day or environmental conditions.
  5. An optional photo or short video can be generated.
  6. A private QR takeaway — sharing as memory, not a gimmick.

Permanent amenity

Caribbean Data Portal

A walkthrough landscape generated from the ecological rhythms of the destination. Quiet at dawn. Alive at noon. Recalibrated for the evening program — same infrastructure.

Can respond to guest movement; wind; tide and lunar phase; sea-surface temperature; sunlight and rainfall; sargassum conditions; coral health; seasonal migration; time of day and hotel programming.

Permanent amenity

Family Discovery

Playful ecology for families: tracks, species, movement and sound — without a conventional kiosk. The room is the interface.

  1. A child steps onto the field. The floor answers with a track.
  2. A hand on the wall gathers particles, flora, a species.
  3. The table holds a living map of the destination — not a touchscreen menu.
  4. The same engine that runs the lobby at dawn. Recalibrated for wonder.

Permanent amenity

Living Water

The pool is not a backdrop. Guest movement becomes light in the water. The wall holds the reef. The same engine as Family Discovery — wet, slower, closer to the body.

  1. A step in the shallows leaves a wake of light.
  2. The reef on the wall can follow tide, season or presence.
  3. Families complete the piece by being in it — not by watching it.

Ecological corridor

  3. Guests walk through the translation, not past a screen.

Wellness Atmospheres

Programmable moment

Brand and product moments

A beverage, fashion or conference partner can occupy the same walls, table and light — for one night — without turning the resort into an advertising screen. The system stays. The content layer changes.

  1. Permanent infrastructure: walls, table, spatial light.
  2. A temporary layer: origin, ritual, product, atmosphere.
  3. By morning, the amenity returns to the destination.

Immersive Dining

Modular system

Inputs. Engine. Outputs.

ANDATA is not selling a single corridor or a single screen. One creative-technology system, specified to the architecture you already have — or the one you are about to build.

Inputs

  • Environmental and marine data
  • On-site sensors
  • Guest movement
  • Music and audio
  • Time and hotel schedules
  • Curated cultural material

Real-time engine

  • TouchDesigner
  • GLSL and generative graphics
  • Depth-camera interaction
  • Custom sensor logic
  • OSC, MIDI, audio analysis
  • Scheduling and content control

Outputs

  • Architectural LED
  • Projection mapping
  • Interactive screens
  • Spatial audio
  • Responsive lighting
  • Private QR takeaways
Operations

Designed to live in the hotel

Art is the guest-facing surface. Reliability is the actual product.

Discreet sensing

Depth cameras and sensors integrated with the architecture. No facial recognition. No identity stored by default.

Edge computer

Local processing for reliable performance when the network is not.

Hotel hours

Scheduled startup and shutdown. Automatic recovery after power or network interruption.

Serviceable hardware

Modular components. Monitoring, calibration and maintenance options.

Seasonal content

Remote updates. One artistic system, different states by season, event or audience.

Staff, not spectacle

Training, operating documentation, and coordination with lighting, acoustic and architectural teams.
